📖 How to Play

  • Goal: Weed your garden until only one plant species remains
  • Drag to connect 3 or more matching plants
  • Connections follow a straight line (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal)
  • Cannot pass through other plants — empty cells are OK
  • After pulling, plants fall down and new seeds sprout from the top
  • Key rule: New plants only sprout from species still in the garden
  • Once a species is fully removed, it never returns
  • If no moves are possible, the garden auto-rearranges
  • Win when only one species remains in your garden!

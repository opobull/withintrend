HBO Max remains the quietest powerhouse in streaming. While rival platforms fight for attention with volume, HBO Max has spent 2026 reinforcing the reputation that built the brand: one prestige series at a time, patiently, with the kind of craft that rewards viewers who actually sit down and pay attention. The service that gave the world *The Sopranos*, *The Wire*, and *Succession* is not coasting on its back catalog. It is quietly assembling one of the strongest year-long slates any streamer has produced this decade.

This guide is based on a comprehensive review of reception across major review aggregators, streaming audience data, and viewer feedback — not a single person's opinion, but a balanced summary of what actual viewers report. That matters, because HBO Max is a platform where hype and reality sometimes diverge, and this roundup is meant to help viewers separate the shows that truly hold up from the ones riding momentum.

Before diving in, one housekeeping note. The streaming service once called HBO Max, then shortened to simply "Max" in 2023, quietly reverted to HBO Max on July 9, 2025. Warner Bros. Discovery chairman Casey Bloys said the company believed the HBO Max name "far better represents our current consumer proposition." Subscribers did not need to do anything — the service, the apps, and the logins all continued to work. Throughout this guide, HBO Max refers to that current (and original) branding. Older articles may still say "Max," but the service is the same.

What follows is a curated look at 20-plus shows grouped by category: the biggest 2026 originals, the prestige classics that still define the platform, comedy highlights, documentary picks, international offerings, and genuinely underrated gems. Each entry covers what the show is about, why it matters, how much of a time commitment it is, and whether it is ongoing or concluded, so viewers can actually plan what to watch next.

## The Biggest New HBO Max Shows of 2026

2026 is the year HBO Max proved its development pipeline is still the strongest in the business. These are the headline originals driving subscriptions and conversation this year.

### A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms

- **Premiere:** January 18, 2026
- **Based on:** George R.R. Martin's *Tales of Dunk and Egg* novellas
- **Genre:** Fantasy adventure
- **Status:** Season 1 complete (6 episodes); Season 2 renewed for 2027
- **Stars:** Peter Claffey, Dexter Sol Ansell, Bertie Carvel, Finn Bennett

The third live-action entry in the *A Song of Ice and Fire* franchise takes a completely different approach from *Game of Thrones* or *House of the Dragon*. Set roughly a century before the events of the main saga, it follows Ser Duncan the Tall, a naive but honorable hedge knight, and his clever young squire Egg, who is secretly a Targaryen prince. Where the earlier Westeros shows leaned on sprawling political intrigue and massive battles, *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* is smaller, warmer, and more charming — closer to a medieval road-trip buddy story than a war epic. Early reception has been strong, with critics praising the chemistry between Peter Claffey and child actor Dexter Sol Ansell, and the show has already been renewed for a second season based on *The Sworn Sword*.

**Why it matters:** It is the first *Game of Thrones* spinoff to deliberately scale down, and it suggests the franchise has room for character pieces as well as epics.

### The Pitt

- **Stars:** Noah Wyle
- **Format:** Real-time medical drama (each episode = one hour of a 15-hour shift)
- **Status:** Season 2 concluded April 2026; Season 3 renewed
- **Seasons available:** 2

*The Pitt* has quietly become one of the most-watched HBO Max series in the platform's history. Season 2, which ran January through April 2026, averaged 15.4 million viewers per episode — a more-than-50-percent increase over Season 1 — with the April 16 finale pulling 9.7 million viewers in its opening weekend alone. Noah Wyle leads the ensemble as Dr. Michael "Robby" Robinavitch, an attending physician at a Pittsburgh trauma center. Each episode covers one hour of his shift in near real time, and Season 2 jumped 10 months forward to an Independence Day shift, folding in themes of burnout, addiction recovery, and institutional rot. Viewers who grew up on *ER* have an easy point of reference, but *The Pitt* is harder-edged, more procedural, and surprisingly quiet when it needs to be.

**Why it matters:** It is the biggest broadcast-style drama on prestige streaming, and it proves that old-fashioned network storytelling still has a massive audience if it is made with care.

### Euphoria Season 3

- **Premiere:** April 12, 2026
- **Stars:** Zendaya, with new additions including Sharon Stone, Natasha Lyonne, Rosalía, Marshawn Lynch, and Danielle Deadwyler
- **Genre:** Contemporary drama
- **Status:** Ongoing; Season 3 currently airing
- **Seasons available:** 3

After a four-year gap, Sam Levinson's polarizing drama finally returns. Season 3 makes a bold creative choice: it jumps five years into the future, aging the cast into early adulthood and reframing the show as a generational coming-of-age story rather than a high school drama. The sprawling new ensemble — including Sharon Stone, Natasha Lyonne, Trisha Paytas, and Spanish superstar Rosalía — signals that HBO Max is once again treating *Euphoria* as an event. Whether viewers love or hate Levinson's style, Season 3 is unavoidable this spring.

**Why it matters:** The return of *Euphoria* is the kind of pop-culture moment that defines a streaming quarter, and the time jump gives the show permission to finally grow up.

### Hacks Season 5

- **Premiere:** April 9, 2026
- **Stars:** Jean Smart, Hannah Einbinder
- **Status:** Final season (10 episodes)
- **Seasons available:** 5

Jean Smart's late-career triumph as legendary Las Vegas comic Deborah Vance concludes with a 10-episode fifth and final season. *Hacks* has been a quiet juggernaut, racking up Emmys across all four previous seasons and consistently ranking as one of the best-reviewed comedies on any platform. The show's central dynamic — an older woman and a younger writer learning from each other without losing their edge — has only deepened, and the final season is being treated as the comedy equivalent of a *Succession* sendoff.

**Why it matters:** *Hacks* is the comedy counterpart to the platform's prestige drama reputation, and ending on the show's terms is exactly the kind of quality signal HBO Max is built on.

### House of the Dragon Season 3

- **Premiere:** June 2026
- **Based on:** George R.R. Martin's *Fire & Blood*
- **Genre:** Epic fantasy
- **Status:** Ongoing (8 episodes in Season 3; renewed for a fourth and final season)

The Targaryen civil war escalates. Season 3 is expected in June 2026, with eight episodes continuing the Dance of the Dragons, and HBO has confirmed that Season 4 will be the show's final run. The first trailer landed in February 2026 and made clear that the long political buildup of Seasons 1 and 2 pays off with large-scale dragon combat. For viewers who drifted away from the slower second season, Season 3 is shaping up as the action-forward payoff.

**Why it matters:** It is the franchise's biggest 2026 release on HBO Max, and it sets up a final season that bookends the *Game of Thrones* era.

### The Last of Us Season 2

- **Premiered:** April 2025 (now streaming in full)
- **Stars:** Pedro Pascal, Bella Ramsey, Kaitlyn Dever, Isabela Merced
- **Status:** Season 2 concluded May 2025; Season 3 in development
- **Episodes:** 7

Season 2 of HBO's prestige video-game adaptation aired in 2025 and is now available on HBO Max as a complete binge. The season jumps five years forward, settling Joel and Ellie into the Jackson, Wyoming commune, and then detonates that peace with one of the most divisive turns in recent prestige TV. Season 3 is in development and most likely lands in the first half of 2027, making Season 2 the current jumping-on point for anyone who has not yet caught up.

**Why it matters:** It is one of the few video-game adaptations taken seriously as drama, and Season 2's full availability makes this the ideal year to watch it back-to-back.

### Industry Season 4

- **Premiered:** Early 2026
- **Genre:** Finance drama
- **Status:** Ongoing

Sometimes described as "Succession for people who actually work in banking," *Industry* continues its slow climb from cult favorite to bona fide prestige drama. Season 4 pushes the show's young investment-bank ensemble further into the kind of moral compromise that defines the genre, and the writing remains razor-sharp. For viewers who want another show about unhappy rich people making each other miserable, this is the most fashionable choice on the platform right now.

### The Dark Wizard

- **Premiere:** April 14, 2026
- **Network:** HBO Original
- **Status:** Upcoming / just launched

One of HBO Max's most-anticipated April drops, *The Dark Wizard* carries the HBO Original label — the designation traditionally reserved for the platform's highest-tier fare. HBO has deliberately kept plot details tight, but the framing, casting, and slot alone put it among the spring's biggest prestige bets.

### DTF St. Louis

- **Format:** Limited series (7 episodes)
- **Stars:** Jason Bateman, David Harbour, Linda Cardellini
- **Genre:** Dark comedy-drama

This seven-episode limited series centers on a love triangle that develops between three people drifting through suburban malaise in St. Louis. The cast alone — Jason Bateman, David Harbour, and Linda Cardellini — makes it a must-sample, and it fits neatly next to *The Leftovers* and *Mare of Easttown* as the kind of character-first limited series HBO Max does better than any competitor.

## Essential HBO Prestige Classics (Still on HBO Max)

These are the catalog anchors — the reason a lot of subscribers never cancel, even in slow months. Every show below is still available on HBO Max in its entirety.

### The Sopranos

- **IMDb:** 9.2/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 92%
- **Episodes:** 86 across 6 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2007)

James Gandolfini's Tony Soprano is arguably the most influential TV character of the last 30 years: a New Jersey mob boss whose panic attacks drive him into therapy, triggering a show that reshaped what television drama could do. *The Sopranos* is funny, brutal, contemplative, and constantly surprising, and the ambiguous final scene has become one of the most debated moments in television history.

**Why it matters:** It is the show that launched the golden age of TV, and everything from *Breaking Bad* to *Succession* is building on its foundation.

### The Wire

- **IMDb:** 9.3/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 94%
- **Episodes:** 60 across 5 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2008)

David Simon's dense, patient examination of Baltimore's institutions — the drug trade, the docks, city hall, the schools, and the press — is frequently named the best television series ever made. It asks for patience in its first three or four episodes and then rewards that patience for five seasons. Each season adds a new institution to the mosaic, and by the end the show has quietly drawn a full portrait of a city and, by extension, of modern America.

### Succession

- **IMDb:** 8.8/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 95%
- **Episodes:** 39 across 4 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2023)
- **Awards:** 19 Emmy wins

Jesse Armstrong's savage corporate tragedy about the Roy family's fight to control a media empire remains the platform's most quotable drama. Jeremy Strong, Sarah Snook, Kieran Culkin, and Brian Cox turn in career work, and the finale is one of the most argued-over endings in prestige TV. The full run is on HBO Max, and it holds up magnificently on rewatch.

### The Leftovers

- **IMDb:** 8.3/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 93%
- **Episodes:** 28 across 3 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2017)

Damon Lindelof and Tom Perrotta's meditation on grief begins after two percent of the world's population suddenly vanishes and spends three seasons asking what happens to the people left behind. Justin Theroux and Carrie Coon deliver two of the decade's most devastating performances, and the final season includes some of the most ambitious hours of television ever produced. It is not a binge for background listening — it wants full attention — but nothing else on HBO Max hits quite like it.

### Band of Brothers

- **IMDb:** 9.4/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 97%
- **Episodes:** 10 (miniseries)
- **Status:** Concluded (2001)

Steven Spielberg and Tom Hanks' World War II miniseries about Easy Company, 101st Airborne, is still the gold standard for wartime television. Based on Stephen Ambrose's nonfiction book, it is 10 episodes of extraordinary craft and genuine emotion that have not aged a day.

### Chernobyl

- **IMDb:** 9.3/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 95%
- **Episodes:** 5 (miniseries)
- **Status:** Concluded (2019)

Craig Mazin's five-episode dramatization of the 1986 nuclear disaster is short, dense, and almost unbearably intense. It is also one of the clearest demonstrations of why HBO Max's limited-series tradition matters — this is the kind of filmmaking that would not exist anywhere else.

### Game of Thrones

- **IMDb:** 9.2/10
- **Episodes:** 73 across 8 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2019)

A divisive ending does not erase what *Game of Thrones* actually accomplished: a decade of appointment television, genuinely cinematic battles, and a central argument that TV could carry the scale and ambition of major fantasy cinema. Rewatching it on HBO Max is also the best possible prep for *House of the Dragon* Season 3 and *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms*.

### True Detective

- **Format:** Anthology
- **Standout seasons:** Season 1 (2014) and *Night Country* (2024)
- **Status:** Ongoing

Each season of *True Detective* tells its own self-contained crime story with a new cast. Season 1, starring Matthew McConaughey and Woody Harrelson, is still the benchmark for atmospheric, philosophical crime TV. The more recent *Night Country*, with Jodie Foster leading, swung the series into horror-tinged Arctic noir and earned broadly positive reviews.

## Best HBO Max Comedies in 2026

The platform's comedy bench has quietly become one of its strongest assets.

### Barry

- **IMDb:** 8.4/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 99%
- **Episodes:** 32 across 4 seasons
- **Status:** Concluded (2023)

Bill Hader's show about a Midwestern hitman who discovers a passion for acting in Los Angeles is a tonal high-wire act that never falls. *Barry* slides between laugh-out-loud comedy, genuinely frightening violence, and moments of real sadness, and its final two seasons are among the most daring storytelling on HBO in years.

### Hacks

- **IMDb:** 8.2/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 98%
- **Seasons:** 5 (final season airing April 2026)

Already covered above under new releases, but worth repeating here: *Hacks* is arguably the best comedy currently airing on any streaming platform. Jean Smart's performance as Deborah Vance has won multiple Emmys, and the writing punches well above its weight.

### The Righteous Gemstones

- **Stars:** Danny McBride, John Goodman, Walton Goggins, Edi Patterson
- **Status:** Recently concluded (2025)

Danny McBride's satire of a televangelist dynasty is richer and more plot-driven than its goofy surface suggests. John Goodman anchors a chaotic ensemble, and the show's finale gave the family a surprisingly poignant sendoff.

### Our Flag Means Death

- **Genre:** Romantic pirate comedy
- **Status:** Concluded after two seasons

A cult favorite: Taika Waititi and Rhys Darby star in a gentle, romantic comedy set in the golden age of piracy. It was canceled before its time, but the two seasons that exist have become comfort viewing for an enormous and devoted audience.

### The Rehearsal

- **Creator:** Nathan Fielder
- **Format:** Docu-comedy
- **Status:** Ongoing

Nathan Fielder's structurally bizarre docu-comedy is genuinely hard to summarize — he stages elaborate real-world rehearsals of other people's life decisions, and the show spirals outward from there. It is among the most original things happening on television, full stop.

### Somebody Somewhere

- **Rotten Tomatoes:** 98%
- **Stars:** Bridget Everett
- **Status:** Concluded (2024)

A warm, small-scale comedy-drama about a woman finding her place in her Kansas hometown. No show on HBO Max has a more loyal fan base, and it is the platform's clearest demonstration that "prestige" does not have to mean "grim."

### Curb Your Enthusiasm

- **Status:** Concluded (2024) after 12 seasons

Larry David's improvised comedy wrapped its 12th and final season in early 2024, and the full 24-year run is on HBO Max. It remains the template for cringe comedy and one of the most rewatchable libraries on the platform.

## Limited Series: The Best Binge Investments

Limited series are HBO Max's specialty — complete stories that begin, end, and do not overstay their welcome.

### Station Eleven

- **IMDb:** 7.8/10 | **Rotten Tomatoes:** 98%
- **Episodes:** 10 (limited series)
- **Based on:** Emily St. John Mandel's novel

Often cited as the most underrated thing HBO Max has ever made. *Station Eleven* follows a group of interconnected characters in the years after a devastating pandemic. It is a show about what makes survival worthwhile rather than survival itself, and the final two episodes are quietly overwhelming.

### Mare of Easttown

- **Rotten Tomatoes:** 94%
- **Episodes:** 7 (limited series)
- **Stars:** Kate Winslet

Kate Winslet's Emmy-winning turn as a small-town Pennsylvania detective grounded one of the best crime miniseries of the decade. The murder mystery is solid; the lived-in sense of place is extraordinary.

### The White Lotus

- **Rotten Tomatoes:** 89%
- **Format:** Anthology

Mike White's satirical anthology set at a rotating roster of luxury resorts became a full-on cultural event. Each season features a new cast and new location, but the barbed social commentary remains constant. The complete run is available and built for back-to-back viewing.

### Half Man

- **Premiere:** Late April 2026
- **Creator:** Richard Gadd
- **Status:** Limited series

Richard Gadd's follow-up to the global phenomenon *Baby Reindeer* is one of the most-anticipated HBO Max limited series of 2026. Expectations are high; Gadd has not yet disappointed.

## Best HBO Max Documentaries Right Now

HBO's documentary tradition goes back decades, and the 2026 slate may be its strongest in years.

### The Alabama Solution

One of the most talked-about documentaries of 2026. This hard-edged exposé on Alabama's prison system digs into overcrowding, systemic violence, political inaction, and the human cost of mass incarceration across decades. Expect awards-season conversation.

### 33 Photos From the Ghetto

A 2026 HBO Original documentary built around a trove of historical photographs from the Warsaw Ghetto, reconstructing the stories behind the images. A sober, careful film in the tradition of HBO's best historical work.

### Mel Brooks: The 99 Year Old Man!

A celebratory career-spanning portrait of Mel Brooks, touching on his return to directing and on his extraordinary longevity in an industry that routinely forgets its elders.

### The Yogurt Shop Murders

A four-part true-crime docuseries on the 1991 Austin, Texas case in which four teenage girls were killed at a yogurt shop. It follows wrongful convictions, appeals, and the decades of doubt that followed, and it is an exemplary piece of patient, morally serious true crime.

### The Earth, Wind & Fire Documentary (Questlove)

HBO has secured the rights to Questlove's documentary on the legendary band Earth, Wind & Fire, with exclusive access to the group's archives. Release in 2026.

## International Highlights on HBO Max

HBO Max's international library is deeper than casual viewers realize, and 2026 continues to add to it.

### Veneno (Spain)

A biographical drama about trans TV personality Cristina Ortiz, "La Veneno." Frequently cited as a landmark of Spanish television and rated 8.7 on IMDb.

### Kamikaze (Denmark)

A Danish drama following an 18-year-old left alone after her family dies in a plane crash. Anchored by Marie Reuther's performance, it is a quiet meditation on grief and wealth.

### Legacy (South Africa)

A family-business drama set against the South African wine industry. Rivalries, secrets, and serialized twists make it an easy recommendation for soapy drama fans.

### My Brilliant Friend (Italy)

The HBO/RAI adaptation of Elena Ferrante's Neapolitan Novels is one of the best foreign-language series of the decade and has an excellent home on HBO Max.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What's new on HBO Max in 2026?

The 2026 lineup is headlined by *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* (January), *The Pitt* Season 2 (January through April), *Industry* Season 4, *Hacks* Season 5 (April), *Euphoria* Season 3 (April), *House of the Dragon* Season 3 (June), and the *Harry Potter* TV adaptation arriving in late 2026 or early 2027. Limited series *DTF St. Louis* and Richard Gadd's *Half Man* round out a slate that is arguably the platform's strongest in years.

### Is HBO Max worth the subscription in 2026?

For viewers who care about prestige drama, limited series, or HBO's back catalog, yes — HBO Max is still the single most valuable streaming subscription on a quality-per-dollar basis. Viewers who primarily want reality, sports, or low-effort comfort TV may get more mileage out of Netflix, Peacock, or Hulu.

### What happened to the Max name change? Is it HBO Max or Max now?

Warner Bros. Discovery rebranded HBO Max to "Max" in 2023 when it folded in Discovery+ content. That decision was reversed on July 9, 2025, when the service was renamed back to HBO Max. WBD cited strong subscriber growth and a desire to "amplify the uniqueness" of HBO's brand. Existing subscriptions, apps, and logins all carried over automatically. In 2026, the correct name is HBO Max.

### What's the best show to start with on HBO Max?

For prestige drama, *The Sopranos* or *Succession*. For something current, *A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms* or *The Pitt*. For comedy, *Barry* or *Hacks*. For a short limited-series commitment, *Chernobyl* (5 episodes), *Station Eleven* (10 episodes), or *Band of Brothers* (10 episodes).

### How much does HBO Max cost in 2026?

HBO Max offers three tiers as of April 2026: Basic with Ads at $10.99/month, Standard (ad-free) at $18.49/month, and Premium (Ultimate, 4K, extra streams) at $22.99/month. Annual plans discount the total by roughly 20 percent. Prices vary by region and are subject to change.

### Does HBO Max have live sports?

HBO Max carries selected live sports, primarily through partnerships inherited from Turner — most notably NCAA basketball (including March Madness coverage), NHL hockey, and select MLB games. Viewers who want comprehensive live-sports coverage generally need to pair HBO Max with a dedicated sports service.

### Can I download HBO Max shows for offline viewing?

Yes. The Standard plan allows up to 30 saved downloads, and the Premium plan raises that to 100. The Basic with Ads plan does not currently support downloads.

### Is The Wire really worth watching in 2026?

Yes. *The Wire* asks for patience in its first three or four episodes, but from the middle of Season 1 onward it is one of the most rewarding viewing experiences any streamer offers. It has only grown in critical stature since it aired, and HBO Max carries the complete 60-episode run.

### What's the Harry Potter TV series on HBO Max?

HBO Max is producing a long-form television adaptation of J.K. Rowling's seven *Harry Potter* novels, with one book per season, helmed by *Succession* writer Francesca Gardiner as showrunner and Mark Mylod as a key director. The premiere window is late 2026 / early 2027. Dominic McLaughlin will play Harry, with John Lithgow as Dumbledore, Janet McTeer as McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu as Snape, and Nick Frost as Hagrid.
